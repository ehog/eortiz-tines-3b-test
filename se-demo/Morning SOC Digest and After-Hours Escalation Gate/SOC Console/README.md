The web app used to drive the demo.

- **Trigger:** `/soc-console`, space auth, `route_type = "webpage"`, pinned on the Links page.
- Renders immediately and polls [Console API](<../Console API/README.md>) every three seconds, so decisions, pages and digests appear as they happen. All writes go through that API; alert injection posts to [Test Alert Intake](<../Test Alert Intake/README.md>) and digest runs to [Run Digest Now](<../Run Digest Now/README.md>).
- Tabs: **Live ops** (counters, event timeline, recent decisions, open pages), **Alert injector** (scenario packs and a custom alert builder with live results), **Gate config** (threshold and suppress-floor sliders, factor weights, coverage window, factor score tables, delivery targets, and a replay button that re-scores the newest alert), **Decision audit** (filterable, expandable score breakdowns and raw alert JSON), **On-call pages** (mock PagerDuty inbox with acknowledge/resolve and the Slack result), **Critical assets** (mock CMDB editor), **Morning digest** (deferred queue, run-now, and the exact email HTML, plain-text and Slack forms that were sent).
- The **Morning digest** tab also carries a **Notifications** card: three toggles (digest email, digest Slack post, Slack page notices) posted as `set_notifications`, and "Send email now" / "Send to Slack now" / "Send both" buttons that post to [Send Digest Notifications](<../Send Digest Notifications/README.md>) with `force`, so a manual send goes out even while a channel is toggled off.
- The header shows the current ET time, whether the gate is armed, and a demo clock override that forces after-hours or business hours; "Reset demo" reseeds a fresh overnight backlog. **Guided tour** launches the narrated walkthrough.
- The guided tour ([Tour.tsx](./Tour.tsx)) is a spotlight overlay of 21 callouts across seven acts: the overnight problem, arming the gate, a Tier 0 alert paging in seconds, noise being suppressed defensibly, retuning the threshold and replaying, the Claude-written morning brief, and the closing 3B value summary. Each callout carries a value headline, what you are looking at, and why it matters.
- It never clicks for you. It dims everything except the target element, tells you exactly which control to press, and advances by itself when the polled state proves the action happened (clock mode changed, a new decision, a page acknowledged, config saved, a replay written, a new digest). "Next anyway" skips a gate, Back steps back, "Exit tour" clears progress. Progress is kept in `sessionStorage`, so a reload resumes; the tour switches tabs itself when the next anchor is elsewhere.
- Anchors are `data-tour="…"` attributes on existing markup — `Card` and `Button` in [ui.tsx](./ui.tsx) accept a `dataTour` prop for this. Renaming or removing an anchor makes that callout fall back to a centred card, so keep the names in sync with `STEPS` in [Tour.tsx](./Tour.tsx).
- Files: [App.tsx](./App.tsx) shell and actions, [api.ts](./api.ts) client and ET time helpers, [ui.tsx](./ui.tsx) primitives, [Tour.tsx](./Tour.tsx) guided tour, one component per tab.
